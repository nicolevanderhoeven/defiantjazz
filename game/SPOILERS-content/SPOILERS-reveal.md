## What really happened
for: all

That afternoon, three people had three separate plans. None of them knew about the others' plans, and nobody planned the blackout.

- **Agnes wants Peggy to find her notes.** Agnes, the sleepwalker, hides notes for her waking self, Peggy, in the elevator's emergency phone panel. On 10-07 she changes the emergency speakers' fail-safe track from the lullaby to *Defiant Jazz*. The next time the power fails, the jazz will wake Peggy, who will walk toward the elevator's emergency light and find the notes.
- **Bertram wants a waffle.** During the Cocoa Hour he sneaks off to the supply room to use his banned waffle iron. It trips the floor's main breaker. It's an accident.
- **Clement wants Hale alone.** Clement is *lucid*: he's the same person at work and at home, and remembers both lives. Hale signed off on the procedure that killed Clement's sister, June. A chat message from Bertram told Clement that Hale would slip off to the elevator during a Cocoa Hour. So Clement times the team's last quota file to trigger one.

At 15:02 the three plans collide. The breaker trips, the speakers play *Defiant Jazz*, and every sleepwalker wakes up as a stranger in the dark. Except Clement. He's still himself, and now the dark hides him. He leaves his badge on the chair by the speaker and walks to the elevator. On the way, in corridor E, he treads on Bertram's dazed waking self ("Sorry, Waffles"). At the elevator, Hale grabs Peggy's wrist ("You can't be here"). She pulls free and runs. Hale picks up the elevator's emergency phone, and seconds later Clement walks in and strangles him. Clement goes back to the speaker and puts his badge back on. Peggy wanders back toward the light, finds Hale and pulls the cable off his neck. At 15:07 the power comes back and everyone falls asleep again. Agnes the sleepwalker comes to in the elevator, holding the cable.

## How it's solved
for: all

**The AI does the heavy lifting.** It pieces the blackout together from data that arrived late. It finds the waffle iron, catches Bertram's lie, finds that Agnes changed the fail-safe track, and accuses her. **It's right that Agnes woke everyone up, and wrong that whoever woke everyone up killed Hale.**

**The human leap.** Someone said "Sorry, Waffles" in the dark, while every sleepwalker was their waking self. A waking self can't remember work. So whoever said it was lucid, and knew Bertram's nickname at work.

- **Not Bertram:** he's the one being apologised to, and he answers like a stranger: "Ow, who's, hello? HELLO?"
- **Not Agnes:** she isn't lucid. She wouldn't need to leave notes for her waking self if her two selves shared memories. At the elevator she's Peggy, shouting her waking name.
- **Clement:** he's the one who calls Bertram "Waffles". And his own interview gives him away: "Someone was screaming by the elevator." That screaming happened only in the dark, and a sleepwalker shouldn't remember the dark at all.

**Clearing Agnes.** Her badge jolts at 15:03:56 as she pulls free. At 15:04:01 Hale picks up the elevator's emergency phone. Nobody does that while still holding on to someone. So the strangling was a new attack, after she'd gone.

**The clues only humans saw:**

- **The nickname:** the ice-pack note "WAFFLES — ice. — C.W.", Agnes's who's-who note, Bertram's "He's the only one who calls me that", and Clement's own "checked on Waffles".
- **The slip:** "Someone was screaming by the elevator", and "Peggy" crossed out on his written statement: a name that was only shouted in the dark.
- **The scarf:** the many-coloured threads in Hale's closed hand and in Mr. Lindqvist's hand match the fringe on Clement's scarf. He didn't stay by the speaker.
- **The napkin:** the napkin from the chair by the speaker has the laundry mark C.A.W.: Clement Arthur Whitlow.
- **The folder:** Mr. Whitlow's photo of the evacuation plan, with his route to the elevator drawn in pen, and "R. Hale" circled on the Procedure Revision 114 clipping.
- **The motive:** June Whitlow's obituary, and Hale's approval of Procedure Revision 114.
