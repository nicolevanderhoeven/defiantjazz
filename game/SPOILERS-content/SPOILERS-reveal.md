## What really happened
for: all

Three people had three separate plans that afternoon. None of them knew about the others' plans, and nobody planned the blackout.

- **Agnes wants Peggy to find her notes.** Agnes's sleepwalker hides notes for her waking self, Peggy, in the elevator's emergency phone panel. On 10-07 she swaps the fail-safe track from the lullaby to *Defiant Jazz*, so the next power cut will wake Peggy, who'll walk toward the elevator's emergency light and find the notes.
- **Bertram wants a waffle.** During the Cocoa Hour he slips off to the supply room to use his contraband waffle iron. It trips the floor's main breaker. It's an accident.
- **Clement wants Hale alone.** Clement is lucid: himself in both lives, with both sets of memories. Hale signed off on the procedure that killed his sister, June. Bertram's chat message told him Hale would slip off to the elevator during a Cocoa Hour, so Clement times the last quota file to trigger one.

At 15:02 the plans collide. The breaker trips, the fail-safe plays *Defiant Jazz*, and every sleepwalker wakes up as a stranger in the dark. Clement doesn't: he's still himself, and now he has the dark for cover. He leaves his badge on the chair by the speaker and walks to the elevator, treading on Bertram's dazed waking self in corridor E on the way ("Sorry, Waffles"). At the elevator, Hale grabs Peggy's wrist ("You can't be here"); she wrenches free and runs. Hale picks up the car's emergency phone, and seconds later Clement walks in and strangles him. Clement goes back to the speaker and puts his badge on again. Peggy drifts back toward the light, finds Hale and pulls the cable off his neck. At 15:07 the power returns, everyone slips back under, and Agnes's sleepwalker comes to holding the cable.

## How it's solved
for: all

**The AI does the heavy lifting.** It rebuilds the blackout from late, buffered data, finds the waffle iron, catches Bertram's lie, finds that Agnes swapped the fail-safe track, and accuses her. **It's right that Agnes woke the floor, and wrong that whoever woke the floor killed Hale.**

**The human leap.** "Sorry, Waffles" was said in the dark, while every sleepwalker was their waking self. A waking self can't reach work memories, so whoever said it was lucid and knew Bertram's work nickname.

- **Not Bertram:** he's the one being apologised to, and he answers like a stranger: "Ow, who's, hello? HELLO?"
- **Not Agnes:** she isn't lucid. Her notes to her waking self only make sense if her two selves don't share memories. At the elevator she's Peggy, shouting her waking name.
- **Clement:** he's the one who calls Bertram "Waffles". And his own testimony gives him away: "Someone was screaming by the elevator." That screaming happened only in the dark, which a sleepwalker shouldn't remember at all.

**Clearing Agnes.** Her badge jolts at 15:03:56 as she breaks free. At 15:04:01 Hale picks up the car's emergency phone, which nobody does while still holding someone. The strangling was a new attack, after she'd gone.

**The clues only humans saw:**

- **The nickname:** the ice-pack note "WAFFLES — ice. — C.W.", Agnes's who's-who note, Bertram's "He's the only one who calls me that", and Clement's own "checked on Waffles".
- **The slip:** "Someone was screaming by the elevator", and the struck-out "Peggy" on his statement, a name only shouted in the dark.
- **The scarf:** the multicoloured threads in Hale's closed hand and in Mr. Lindqvist's hand match Clement's fringe. He wasn't by the speaker.
- **The napkin:** the cloth from the chair by the speaker carries the laundry mark C.A.W., Clement Arthur Whitlow.
- **The folder:** Mr. Whitlow's photo of the evacuation plan with his route to the elevator traced in pen, and "R. Hale" circled on the Procedure Revision 114 clipping.
- **The motive:** June Whitlow's obituary, and Hale's approval of Procedure Revision 114.
