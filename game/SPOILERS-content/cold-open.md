# Cold open

## The Defiant Jazz Incident
*The power went out for five minutes. When it came back, the supervisor was dead in the elevator, and the woman standing over him was holding the cable that killed him. For all five minutes in the dark, every speaker in the building had been playing jazz.*

> Incident File SF-1008
> KIPSAKE · SLEEP FLOOR · OFFICE OF FLOOR SECURITY
> Subject: Death of Mr. Reginald Hale, Floor Supervisor (day staff)
> Date: 2026-10-08

Kipsake, a firm in the seaside town of Port Quiet, pays its staff to sleep while their **sleepwalking selves** do the work. At work, each of them is a **sleepwalker**, who remembers only work. At home, they're their **waking self**, who remembers only home. A sleepwalker knows its desk and colleagues; its waking self has never seen them. Day staff, like the supervisor, Mr. Hale, stay awake at work. Every sleepwalker wears a badge, and the doors log each badge that passes. But a badge can't tell who's wearing it.

**What happened**

- **14:58.** One team, Deep Sleep Operations (DSO), meets its quota for the quarter. Its reward is a Cocoa Hour: cocoa, and no music. It starts at 15:00 in Break Room 2, with all six DSO clerks and Mr. Hale.
- **About 15:02.** The power goes out. Every speaker on the floor starts playing jazz, and the jazz wakes everyone up: for five minutes, every sleepwalker is their ordinary waking self, in the dark, somewhere they've never seen.
- **15:07.** The power comes back and the jazz stops. Everyone falls back asleep and remembers none of it. Mr. Hale is found strangled in the elevator, which is stopped at the Sleep Floor with its doors open. A DSO clerk, Agnes K., is in the elevator with him, holding a cable.

Floor Security has named three suspects, all DSO clerks: **Agnes K.**, **Bertram L.** and **Clement W.**
