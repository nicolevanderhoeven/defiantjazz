# Cold open

## The Defiant Jazz Incident
*The power went out for five minutes. When it came back, the supervisor was dead in the elevator, and the woman standing over him was holding the cable that killed him. For all five minutes in the dark, every speaker in the building had been playing jazz.*

> Incident File SF-1008
> KIPSAKE · SLEEP FLOOR · OFFICE OF FLOOR SECURITY
> Subject: Death of Mr. Reginald Hale, Floor Supervisor (day staff)

Kipsake, a firm in the seaside town of Port Quiet, pays its staff to sleep while their **sleepwalking selves** do the work. At work, each of them is a **sleepwalker**, who remembers only work. At home, they're their **waking self**, who remembers only home. A sleepwalker knows its desk and colleagues; its waking self has never seen them. Day staff, like the supervisor, Mr. Hale, stay awake at work. Every sleepwalker wears a badge, and the doors log each badge that passes. But a badge can't tell who's wearing it.

**What happened**

- **14:58.** One team, Deep Sleep Operations (DSO), met its quota for the quarter. Its reward was a Cocoa Hour: a break from work to sit together and drink hot cocoa, with no music. It started at 15:00 in Break Room 2, with all six DSO clerks and Mr. Hale.
- **About 15:02.** The power went out. Every speaker on the floor started playing jazz, and the jazz woke everyone up: for five minutes, every sleepwalker was their ordinary waking self, in the dark, somewhere they'd never seen.
- **15:07.** The power came back and the jazz stopped. Everyone fell back asleep and remembered none of it. Mr. Hale was found strangled in the elevator, which was stopped at the Sleep Floor with its doors open. A DSO clerk, Agnes K., was in the elevator with him, holding a cable.

Floor Security has named three suspects, all DSO clerks: **Agnes K.**, **Bertram L.** and **Clement W.**

**Your role**

You're an investigator, brought in to work alongside Ms. Okonjo of Floor Security. The Directors want a name by 17:00, before the shift goes home. Your job is to name who killed Mr. Hale and explain why.

- **ask**: your AI assistant has read Kipsake's records, but it has never spoken to anyone. Ask it anything.
- **investigate**: Ms. Okonjo can follow up three leads for you, in order: a crime scene, a visit to a suspect at home, and one last interview. Each time, you pick one of three options, and you'll only see the one you pick. Use them whenever you like, between questions to the AI. You can accuse before you've used them all.
- **accuse**: when you're ready, name the killer and say why.
