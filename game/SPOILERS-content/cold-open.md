# Cold open

## The Defiant Jazz Incident
*At 15:07 on 8 October, the power came back on Kipsake's Sleep Floor. Floor Supervisor Reginald Hale was dead in the elevator, and a sleepwalker stood over him with the cable in her hands. For the five minutes before that, every speaker on the floor had been playing Defiant Jazz.*

> Incident File SF-1008
> KIPSAKE · SLEEP FLOOR · OFFICE OF FLOOR SECURITY
> Subject: Death of Mr. Reginald Hale, Floor Supervisor (day staff)
> Date: 2026-10-08

Kipsake, a firm in the seaside town of Port Quiet, pays its staff to sleep while their **sleepwalkers** do the work. A sleepwalker remembers only work and the **waking self** only home: a sleepwalker knows its desk and colleagues, but its waking self has never seen them. Day staff, like Mr. Hale, are never put under. Every sleepwalker wears a badge that door readers track, but a badge doesn't know who's wearing it.

**Any music except Kipsake's lullaby wakes everyone who hears it** as their waking self. When the music stops they slip back under, and the sleepwalker remembers nothing of the waking: the lights blinked, and it was somewhere else.

**What happened**

- **14:58.** Deep Sleep Operations (DSO) hits its quarterly quota and earns a Cocoa Hour, a quiet reward with cocoa and no music. It starts at 15:00 in Break Room 2, with all six DSO clerks and Mr. Hale.
- **About 15:02.** The Sleep Floor loses main power. The emergency speakers should play the lullaby; instead they play *Defiant Jazz* on every speaker until the power returns.
- **15:07.** Power returns and the jazz stops. Mr. Hale is found strangled in the elevator car, held at the Sleep Floor with its doors open. Agnes K. is in the car with him, holding a cable.

Floor Security has named three persons of interest, all from DSO: **Agnes K.**, **Bertram L.** and **Clement W.**
