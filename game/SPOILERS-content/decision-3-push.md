# Decision 3 · Push one sleepwalker
prompt: The Directors want a name by 17:00, before the shift wakes up and goes home. Ms. Okonjo has time for one more interview. Who do we push?

## Agnes
option: Agnes
note: ask about the notes in the phone panel
aliases: agnes k., agnes k, agnes, a

> 16:30 · Kipsake · Security interview
[Agnes sits on the chair, lanyard on, her right wrist in a crepe bandage from the medical exam. Ms. Okonjo's gloved hand passes her four notes in evidence bags. One is the note from Mr. Hale's jacket, which begins "If you are reading this, you are me…" and ends "Don't trust them."]
Ms. Okonjo: These were in the elevator phone panel.
Agnes: They're for her. The other me.
[She takes the folded note from behind her badge card and adds it to the pile.]
Ms. Okonjo: Why?
Agnes: So she knows who to trust.
[Teary, she holds one bag up to the camera. In joined-up handwriting, with "Waffles" underlined:]
> WHO'S WHO. Dorothy: kind. Fern: hums. BERTRAM: tells Mr. Hale things. Careful. (Clement calls him 'Waffles'.) Clement: kind to me.

## Bertram
option: Bertram
note: ask why he lied about the cocoa
aliases: bertram l., bertram l, bertram, b

> 16:30 · Kipsake · Security interview
[Bertram sits on the chair, lanyard on, the ice pack on his shoulder.]
Ms. Okonjo: You weren't having cocoa, Mr. L.
[His shoulders slump.]
Bertram: Fine. I was making a waffle.
Ms. Okonjo: And who brought you the ice?
[He turns the ice pack to the camera. The sticky note, in block capitals: "WAFFLES — ice. — C.W."]
Bertram: He's the only one who calls me that.

## Clement
option: Clement
note: ask what he did after the lights came back, in writing
aliases: clement w., clement w, clement, c

> 16:30 · Kipsake · Security interview
[Clement sits on the chair, lanyard and scarf on, a Kipsake "Incident Statement" form on a clipboard in his lap.]
Ms. Okonjo: For the record, Mr. W. In your own hand. What did you do after the lights came back?
[He writes in silence. The form, in block capitals, line by line:]
> 15:07 Lights returned. I was seated by the speaker.
> I went to the corridor and checked on Waffles (shoulder).
> I hope ~~Peggy~~ Agnes is being looked after.
[The word "Peggy" has a single line through it and is still legible. His pen hovers over it for a beat. His hands are clean. He holds the clipboard out to Ms. Okonjo.]
Clement: Is that all?
