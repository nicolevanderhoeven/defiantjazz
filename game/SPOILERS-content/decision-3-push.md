# Decision 3 · Push one sleepwalker
prompt: Kipsake's directors want a name by 17:00, before the shift wakes up and goes home. Ms. Okonjo has time for one more interview. Who do we push?

## Agnes
option: Agnes
note: ask about the notes in the elevator's phone panel
aliases: agnes k., agnes k, agnes, a

> 16:30 · Kipsake · Security interview
[Agnes sits on the chair, wearing her lanyard. Her right wrist is in a crepe bandage from the medical exam. Ms. Okonjo's gloved hand passes her four notes in evidence bags. One of them is the note found in Mr. Hale's jacket. It begins "If you are reading this, you are me…" and ends "Don't trust them."]
Ms. Okonjo: These were in the elevator phone panel.
Agnes: They're for her. The other me.
[She takes the folded note from behind her badge card and adds it to the pile.]
Ms. Okonjo: Why?
Agnes: So she knows who to trust.
[In tears, she holds one bag up to the camera. The note in it is in joined-up handwriting, with "Waffles" underlined:]
> WHO'S WHO. Dorothy: kind. Fern: hums. BERTRAM: tells Mr. Hale things. Careful. (Clement calls him 'Waffles'.) Clement: kind to me.

## Bertram
option: Bertram
note: ask why he lied about the cocoa
aliases: bertram l., bertram l, bertram, b

> 16:30 · Kipsake · Security interview
[Bertram sits on the chair, wearing his lanyard, the ice pack on his shoulder.]
Ms. Okonjo: You weren't having cocoa, Mr. L.
[His shoulders slump.]
Bertram: Fine. I was making a waffle.
Ms. Okonjo: And who brought you the ice?
[He turns the ice pack toward the camera. The sticky note says, in block capitals: "WAFFLES — ice. — C.W."]
Bertram: He's the only one who calls me that.

## Clement
option: Clement
note: ask him to write down what he did after the lights came back
aliases: clement w., clement w, clement, c

> 16:30 · Kipsake · Security interview
[Clement sits on the chair, wearing his lanyard and scarf. On his lap is a clipboard with a Kipsake "Incident Statement" form.]
Ms. Okonjo: For the record, Mr. W. In your own hand. What did you do after the lights came back?
[He writes without speaking. What he writes on the form, in block capitals, line by line:]
> 15:07 Lights returned. I was seated by the speaker.
> I went to the corridor and checked on Waffles (shoulder).
> I hope ~~Peggy~~ Agnes is being looked after.
[The word "Peggy" is crossed out with a single line, but you can still read it. His pen hovers over it for a moment. His hands are clean. He holds out the clipboard to Ms. Okonjo.]
Clement: Is that all?
