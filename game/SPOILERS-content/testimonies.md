# Opening testimonies

## T-A · Agnes K. · 15:30
[Security interview. Agnes K. sits on a chair against a plain wall, a Kipsake lanyard and badge card around her neck. She wears a plain top and no scarf. Ms. Okonjo of Floor Security asks the questions from off camera.]
Ms. Okonjo: Ms. K. Where were you when the lights went out?
Agnes: Having cocoa. By the window. Then I was in the elevator, holding a cable.
Ms. Okonjo: And Mr. Hale?
Agnes: I think I was trying to get it off him?
[She rubs her palms together, knuckles out. She doesn't hold eye contact.]
[While Ms. Okonjo's pen scratches, Agnes slides a folded slip of paper behind the badge card in her lanyard holder.]
Agnes: Will someone be taking apart the elevator? The phone panel, I mean?

## T-B · Bertram L. · 15:40
[Security interview. Bertram L. sits on the same chair, lanyard on, holding an ice pack to his right shoulder. A yellow sticky note is stuck to the ice pack. There's a smudge of flour on the back of his free hand.]
Ms. Okonjo: Mr. L. Mr. Hale met you this morning?
Bertram: He said he'd step out during the Cocoa Hour. Something to pick up.
[He looks down and to the left.]
Ms. Okonjo: And when the lights went?
Bertram: I was having cocoa.
[He avoids eye contact.]
Bertram: Then I was in the corridor. Sore shoulder. Sheep everywhere.
[He shifts the ice pack, and for a moment the sticky note faces the camera. In block capitals: "WAFFLES — ice. — C.W."]

## T-C · Clement W. · 15:20
[Security interview. Clement W. sits on the same chair, lanyard on, hands folded in his lap. He wears a fringed scarf, looped once so both ends hang down his chest. Its fringe threads are many different colours. His hands are clean.]
Ms. Okonjo: Mr. W. Where were you when the lights went out?
Clement: A wonderful afternoon, until it wasn't.
Clement: The lights blinked, and I was by the speaker. Same as everyone, I expect.
Clement: Someone was screaming by the elevator.
[He pauses for two beats, then straightens his lanyard.]
Clement: Terrible about Mr. Hale.
